VELIRRA AURA OUD

Inspired by Ameer Al Oud

Velirra Aura Oud is a luxurious amber-woody fragrance inspired by Ameer Al Oud. A rich, smoky oud fragrance with warm vanilla sweetness. It opens with bold woody and oud accords, develops into a smooth gourmand vanilla heart, and settles into a creamy sandalwood base with a luxurious amber warmth.
