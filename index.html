Velirra Delivery Policy

All orders are processed and delivered within 1-3 business days. Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location and the courier service.

Once your order has been delivered, you will receive a confirmation message with tracking details, where available. Velirra is not responsible for delays caused by courier services, weather conditions, incorrect shipping information, or any circumstances beyond our control.
